# The Ship of Theseus — What we carry forward

The `theseus-ship` edition now contains the new production with Mara, Pip and Ione: fourteen scenes in each language, 206 recorded speech clips and four selected song takes. It replaces the earlier short production at the existing demo address.

The ship uses painted hulls and harbour/sea inserts. A separate harbour foreground sits above ships and below all character hierarchies. Both languages have navigation scores with dialogue, song form and lyric anchors. Shared AniMus changes include two collapsible effects lanes, audio-library import/export, failure-isolated playback and word-anchored song mouth poses, including duet allocation. CutOut atlas cropping and caption audio-language selection are shared source changes.

Songs are existing recordings. Sixty-seven requested lyric lines have automatic word anchors; five requested lines remain unlocated. Letter-based mouth shapes and singer allocation follow the manuscript and require further musical review. No new musical recordings or recorded effects collection is claimed. Real iPad acceptance remains pending.

Production collections use content-specific URL keys. Plugin distributions are rebuilt from shared sources, and Forge catalog/registry/homepage metadata is updated together.

## Dansk

Editionen indeholder nu den nye forestilling med Mara, Pip og Ione: 14 scener på hvert sprog, 206 taleoptagelser og fire sangtakes. Den afløser den tidligere korte forestilling på den eksisterende demoadresse.

Skibet har malede skrog og indklip fra hav og havn. Havnens hus og træ ligger foran skibene og bag alle figurdele. Navigationspartiturerne indeholder replikker, sangform og sanglinjer. De fælles kilder indeholder effektsporene, bibliotekets import/eksport, afspilning uafhængigt af lydfejl samt sangmundstillinger og duetfordeling.

De eksisterende sangoptagelser er bevaret. 67 bestilte tekstlinjer har automatiske ordankre; fem er endnu ikke lokaliseret. Mundstillinger og sangerfordeling følger manuskriptet og kræver fortsat musikalsk gennemgang. Der er ikke lavet nye musikoptagelser eller en ny samling af indspillede effekter. En rigtig iPad-prøve udestår.
