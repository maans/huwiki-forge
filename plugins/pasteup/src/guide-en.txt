! PasteUp — your creative workshop

Make posters, collages and interactive boards with the original PasteUp fields.

# Open [[PasteUp]] and create your own retro poster or TWingo board.
# Unlock the board with its padlock. Double-click an element to edit it.
# Drag elements and corners to change position and size. The toolbox controls layers, appearance and click actions.
# Save the whole wiki with TiddlyWiki’s Save button.

!! Pictures and sharing

Import images with TiddlyWiki’s normal import. Use an embedded image in a field, for example `<$image source="My image"/>`. The image is then included in the saved HTML wiki; no Drive account is required.

The print button opens a version for paper or your browser’s PDF printer. The poster’s download button exports its data; save the whole wiki for a complete copy with images. Slideshow can use the board as a slide, background or interactive layout.
